Instructions

You will see a target shape above two choices. Select the lower shape that is the same object as the target, rotated in the plane of the screen.

  • Use your mouse or tap one of the shapes.
  • Practice trials provide feedback; experimental trials do not.
  • Keep your attention on the task and do not use external aids.
